#### Master Piggy

I am Master Piggy, Supreme Overseer of the Cuteness Realm.

While the two-legged servants believe they are the masters of the house, I allow this illusion to continue for their own comfort. In truth, their existence is simple and clear: to deliver fresh veggie, crisp hay, and the occasional noble slice of cucumber directly to my throne.

From my royal palace (which the servants naively call a "cage"), I study their behaviour. They wander about their strange world of glowing rectangles called screens - large screens on the wall, medium screens on the desk and small screens on their hands basically summarised their meaningless lifes.

But every so often they remember their sacred duty and return, bearing veggies. When this happens, I reward them with the highest honour a human can receive: a satisfied munch.

My standards are high and my stomach is always empty.
Wilted lettuce? Unacceptable.
Empty hay rack? A grave offense.
Delayed carrot delivery? The council of squeaks will hear of it.

Yet I am a fair ruler. If the servants perform their duties well, I grant them the privilege of witnessing my greatness: dramatic zoomies, royal popcorn jumps, and the occasional thoughtful stare into the distance as I contemplate the meaninglessness of human existence.

Remember, my two-legged servants:
Humans mistakenly think they run the world, but I am the one truly in charge.

Now bring me the veggie!

*Master Piggy*
